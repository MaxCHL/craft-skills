# Opportunity Brief

Written at the earliest stage — before committing to build anything.
Answers: "Is this worth doing?" and "How big is the problem?"

---

## When to Write

- A new product area or market is being explored
- A significant user pain point has been identified
- Leadership or stakeholders need alignment before investment
- A discovery sprint has surfaced an insight worth pursuing

---

## Template

```markdown
# [Opportunity Name] — Opportunity Brief

**Status:** Exploring | Validating | Approved | Parked
**Author:** [Name]
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

---

## Problem

> One paragraph. What pain does the user experience today? Use user research, support data,
> NPS verbatims, or analytics evidence. Not a solution description.

[User-centric problem statement with evidence]

**Evidence:**
- [Data point / user quote / support ticket volume]
- [Analytics: e.g. "42% of users drop off at step 3"]

---

## Who Has This Problem

| Persona | Frequency | Severity | Evidence |
|---------|-----------|----------|----------|
| [Role] | [Daily / Weekly / Rare] | [Blocking / Annoying / Minor] | [Source] |

---

## Success Metrics

> Define what "solved" looks like before designing the solution.

| Metric | Current Baseline | Target | Timeframe |
|--------|-----------------|--------|-----------|
| [Metric name] | [Value] | [Value] | [e.g. 3 months post-launch] |

---

## Opportunity Size

**Impact if solved:** [High / Medium / Low] — [1-2 sentence justification]
**Affected users:** [Estimated % or absolute number]
**Strategic fit:** [How this aligns with product/company goals]

---

## Solution Space (Hypotheses Only)

> Do NOT design the solution here. List hypotheses to validate.

- Hypothesis A: [If we do X, users will Y]
- Hypothesis B: [Alternative approach worth exploring]

---

## Appetite

> From Shape Up: how much time/resource is this problem worth?

[Small batch (1-2 weeks) / Standard (4-6 weeks) / Large (quarter+)]
Justification: [Why this appetite matches the opportunity size]

---

## Risks & Unknowns

> 🐇 **Rabbit Hole:** [What could make this harder than it looks]

- [Risk / Unknown] — [What needs to be validated before proceeding]

---

## Non-Goals (What We Won't Do)

- 🚫 [What this opportunity explicitly excludes]
- 🚫 [Adjacent problem we are NOT solving]

---

## Open Questions

> ⚠️ **Open Question:** [Question] — Owner: [Name] — Due: [Date]

---

## Recommendation

[ ] Proceed to Epic definition
[ ] Run a discovery spike first — [specify what to validate]
[ ] Park — [reason]
[ ] Reject — [reason]
```

---

## What Makes a Good Opportunity Brief

| Good | Bad |
|------|-----|
| Problem grounded in user evidence | "We should build X" (solution-first) |
| Success metrics defined upfront | "We'll measure success later" |
| Appetite set before design begins | Unbounded scope from the start |
| Non-Goals explicit | Implied scope that grows during execution |
| Hypotheses, not designs | Wireframes in the brief (too early) |
